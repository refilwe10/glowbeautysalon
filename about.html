Our Journey

A Dream Turned Into Reality

Glow Beauty Salon was founded in 2022 by three passionate aestheticians – Elena, Maria, and Sofia. The name "Glow" reflects our philosophy: to help every client feel radiant and confident from the inside out.

What started as a small pop-up in a co-working space quickly grew into a beloved local gem. Our loyal clients loved our personalised approach and our commitment to using only premium, cruelty-free products.

In early 2024, we expanded to our own standalone studio — designed to be a serene sanctuary in the heart of the city.
