## Project Overview
A professional, elegant, responsive website for Glow Beauty Salon — a luxury beauty salon offering hair, nail, makeup, and facial treatments. The site is designed to establish a strong online presence, showcase services with pricing, and provide an effortless booking experience for clients.

## Website Goals and Objectives
1. Increase service enquiries by 30% within two months of launch
2. Establish Glow as a luxury and expert beauty brand
3. Showcase a stunning visual portfolio of beauty work
4. Provide a seamless booking experience through an elegant, easy-to-use form

## Key Features and Functionality
- Elegant, image-driven homepage with full-width hero background image
- About page with team profiles, salon statistics, and brand philosophy
- Services page with category images and interactive "View Pricing" toggle
- Enhanced booking form with grouped sections, sidebar info cards, and validation feedback
- Contact page with two salon locations
- Fully responsive design with hamburger menu on mobile
- Google Fonts typography (Playfair Display + Lato)
- Smooth hover effects, transitions, and pseudo-class styling throughout

## Off-Page SEO Strategy (Part 3 Theory)
To further improve the SEO of Glow Beauty Salon, the following off-page strategies will be implemented post-launch:
- **Local SEO:** Claim and optimize the Google Business Profile for both Johannesburg locations.
- **Social Media:** Link the website to active Instagram and Facebook profiles, ensuring NAP (Name, Address, Phone) consistency.
- **Backlinks:** Partner with local beauty bloggers and influencers for reviews and links back to the Glow Beauty Salon website.
